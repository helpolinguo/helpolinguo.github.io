Tapping out the times for La Skopo

Press space on each line as it begins. → skips a line, and skipped lines are worked out from the tapped ones either side.

Eight taps are enough: the first and the last line of each stanza. The song plays a few bars between one stanza and the next, and a tap on either side of that music keeps it out of the working-out — the six lines between two taps are spread over the stanza they belong to and not over the interlude. Tapping every line is exact; tapping only the four stanza openings is not enough, because then each interlude is spread across the stanza before it.

← takes back the last tap, ↓ rewinds five seconds, enter plays and pauses, R starts over. Then copy the block at the foot over the one in index.html.
